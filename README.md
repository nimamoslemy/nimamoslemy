## 🔬 About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering at **Heriot-Watt University** (Edinburgh, UK), working on an **EPSRC-funded programme**. My research and engineering work bridge physical manufacturing and intelligent software — combining **Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF)**, **architected lattice structures**, **finite element analysis (FEA)**, and **deep learning for defect detection** alongside native **C++20 / C# / Python** desktop AI tools.
