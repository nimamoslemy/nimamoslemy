## 🔬 About me

I'm a PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering at **Heriot-Watt University**, working on an **EPSRC-funded project**. My research brings together **laser powder bed fusion (LPBF)**, **lattice structures**, **finite element analysis**, and **machine learning for defect detection** in additive manufacturing.

## 🧭 Research focus

- 🖨️ **Metal additive manufacturing** — LPBF, process quality, and defect detection
- 🧩 **Lattice structures and metamaterials** — mechanical behaviour and simulation
- 🧠 **AI for engineering** — machine learning, deep learning, and image analysis
- 🧬 **Biomedical manufacturing** — bioprinting, scaffolds, and patient-specific prostheses
