# Hi, I'm Nima Moslemy 👋

### PhD Researcher · Additive Manufacturing · Mechanical Simulation · AI

I'm a PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering at **Heriot-Watt University**, Edinburgh, working on an **EPSRC-funded project**. My research interests bring together laser powder bed fusion (LPBF), lattice structures, finite element analysis, and machine learning for defect detection in additive manufacturing.

[Portfolio](https://nimamoslemy.github.io/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nimamoslemy/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oj20_dkAAAAJ&hl=en) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Nima-Moslemy) · [Email](mailto:nm4015@hw.ac.uk)

## Research focus

- **Metal additive manufacturing:** LPBF, process quality, and defect detection
- **Lattice structures and metamaterials:** mechanical behaviour and simulation
- **AI for engineering:** machine learning, deep learning, and image analysis
- **Biomedical manufacturing:** bioprinting, scaffolds, and patient-specific prostheses
